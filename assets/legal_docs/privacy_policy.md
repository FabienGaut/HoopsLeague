Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30/11/2025

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how Hoopsleague (hereinafter referred to as the "App", "we", "us", or "our"), developed by Fabien Gautier (Individual Developer), collects, uses, and protects user information.

This app provides free basketball prediction challenges with virtual points. It is for entertainment purposes only and does not involve real money or paid rewards.

By using our App, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

2. Information We Collect

We collect two types of data:

2.1. Information You Provide to Us

To create an account and use the features for saving your points and predictions, we collect:

Email Address: Used as a unique identifier, for password recovery, and for important communications.

Password (Securely Hashed): Managed by Supabase. Your password is encrypted and salted; it is never accessible to us in plain text.

Username / Nickname: To display in the game Leaderboards.

2.2. Information Collected Automatically

When you use the App, certain technical data may be collected automatically by the system or third-party services to ensure the proper functioning and security of the service:

IP Address and Server Logs: For server security and fraud prevention (e.g., preventing cheating in leaderboards).

Technical and Usage Data: Technical information (such as IP address, device type, and server logs) may be collected for security purposes, fraud prevention, and to ensure the proper functioning of the App.

Usage Data: Interactions with the application (matches selected, points won, rankings) may be analyzed in an aggregated and anonymized manner to improve the user experience.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information for the following purposes:

• Account Management  
• Game Operation  
• Security and Fraud Prevention  
• Service Improvement  
• Legal Compliance (only if required by law)


Legal Compliance: To comply with legal obligations (only if required by law).

5. Your Rights and Data Deletion

In accordance with the GDPR (Europe), CCPA (USA), and App Store/Play Store requirements:

Right to Access and Rectify: You can view or modify your profile information directly within the App settings.

GDPR Rights: Users also have the right to restrict processing, object to processing, and request data portability.

Right to Delete (Right to be Forgotten): You have the right to request the complete deletion of your account and all associated data.

How to delete: A "Delete My Account" option is available in the App settings. You can also contact us at support@hoopsleague.fr to make this request. Once processed, your data (email, points, history) will be permanently erased from our servers (Supabase).

6. Data Security

We implement reasonable security measures to protect your information. Passwords are hashed using Supabase industry-standard security (bcrypt or similar) and all communications are encrypted via HTTPS. However, no method of transmission over the Internet is 100% secure.

7. Children's Privacy

Our App is not intended for children under the age of 13 (or the minimum age required in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If we discover that a child has provided us with personal information, we will delete it immediately.

Note on Game Content: This app is a sports prediction game designed for a general audience. It relies on sports knowledge and statistics.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page. Users will be notified in-app in case of major changes.
